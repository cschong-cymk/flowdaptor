Bridging the gap in business matching — with a veteran who's seen it all

Most business matching today is still stuck in the Rolodex era. Who you know. Which WhatsApp group you're in. Whether someone remembers to make the introduction.

That's why we are excited to be working with TK Yap, CFA, a seasoned veteran of the financial industry, to build DealKaki — a tool that makes business matching systematic, not serendipitous.

TK brings decades of front-line experience: he's seen the friction firsthand. We're combining that domain wisdom with technology to solve a real, persistent problem — connecting the right people to the right opportunities, at scale.

Early days, but the vision is clear: business matching that doesn't depend on luck.

If you've ever struggled to find the right partner, supplier, or investor — we'd love to hear your war stories. What's broken in business matching today?
